TNEA Counselling 2027

STEP BY STEP

1 Register on tneaonline.org with +2 marks

2 Pay registration fee ₹500 (OC) ₹250 (SC/ST)

3 Certificate verification at TFC centres - online

4 Fill choice - Max 200+ colleges, order wisely!

5 Seat allotment - 3 rounds + supplementary

6 Pay tuition & download allotment order

7 Report to college with originals

DOCUMENTS CHECKLIST

📄 12th Marksheet

📄 Transfer Certificate

📄 Community Certificate (if BC/MBC/SC)

📄 Nativity Certificate

📄 First Graduate Certificate (if applicable)

📄 Aadhar Card

📄 Passport Photos (6)

❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid

• Putting low preference college at top - You will get it and lose better chance!

• Not including safe backup colleges - Always add 10-15 safe options

• Wrong community certificate upload - Use latest TN govt format

• Missing certificate verification deadline - Check SMS/email daily

• Not paying allotment fee in time - Seat will be cancelled automatically

• Forgetting First Graduate certificate - You lose ₹25k/year benefit

✅ Expert Tips from Madhankumar

• Fill minimum 80-100 choices - Mix of dream, reachable & safe

• Order: Dream colleges (cutoff +2) → Reachable (cutoff ±1) → Safe (cutoff -2 to -4)

• Check last year closing department-wise before filling - Don't rely on college reputation alone

• CSE in Tier-2 college is better than Mech in Tier-1 for IT jobs - Choose branch wisely

• Keep all originals + 3 sets xerox ready before counselling starts
